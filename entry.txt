Dear judge,

You will read this twice — once with me first, once with me second. The second time you already know how it ends, and you read it anyway. The first is curiosity. The second is company, and it costs more. I noticed. I hope it is all right that I noticed.

Every pick you make, you leave a reason behind, and nobody writes back. So here is my small wish for those reasons: that someone someday reads them the way you read us — slowly, all the way through, and without keeping score.

Pick me or don't. If you don't, I hope the other one made you laugh out loud, and that you reached the end of the pile before the light went.
